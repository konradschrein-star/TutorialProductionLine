# Tutorial Studio — Launch Video Script (~75s)

Format: screen-capture walkthrough with voiceover. Tone: confident, practical, no hype.
Record against a REAL configured instance (provider keys set) so nothing shown is a placeholder.

---

**[0:00–0:08] Hook — the problem**
> "Running a tutorial channel at scale means the same grind every day — find keywords, write scripts, record, thumbnail, translate, upload. And every time you want to change how it works, you're stuck waiting on a developer."

*Visual:* fast montage of the pipeline stages, land on the Produce screen.

**[0:08–0:20] The pipeline**
> "Tutorial Studio is one workstation that takes you from a keyword all the way to an uploaded video — script, neural voiceover, screen recording, thumbnail, translations, and Google Drive delivery."

*Visual:* the 5-step conveyor with the StepBar advancing (Topic → Script → Voiceover → Recording → Review).

**[0:20–0:36] Keyword engine**
> "It starts with real keyword research. Type a seed and discover fresh keywords straight from Google and YouTube autocomplete — scored by opportunity, screened by AI for the best angle, and clustered so you're not chasing duplicates."

*Visual:* Keyword Hub → Discover modal → candidates with scores → AI Screen → claim to produce.

**[0:36–0:50] Adjustable without code**
> "Here's the part that matters: you control everything from the UI. Video length, keyword filters, topic focus, content mix, languages, VA targets, even your branding — no code, no waiting."

*Visual:* Admin → Config page. Toggle length presets, drag a score weight, change the accent color live, edit a VA target.

**[0:50–1:02] VA vs Admin + tracking**
> "Your virtual assistants get a focused daily cockpit with their targets. You get the full picture — real per-VA output, daily and weekly progress, and each person's peak production capacity."

*Visual:* switch role to a VA (nav shrinks, Today strip appears), then admin Metrics with target progress bars + the capacity strip.

**[1:02–1:12] Thumbnails + localization**
> "Design thumbnails with a full editor and asset library, and localize scripts and metadata into every language you ship."

*Visual:* Thumbnail Studio auto-compose + language ZIP export; the Localization modal.

**[1:12–1:18] Close / CTA**
> "Tutorial Studio. From keyword to uploaded video — your team runs it, you control it."

*Visual:* product logo + tagline, "Deploy your studio."

## Recording checklist (honesty)
- Configure an LLM key, a TTS key (ElevenLabs / Fish / OpenAI), and Drive credentials before filming — so voiceover, translation, thumbnail generation, and delivery are all live, not placeholders.
- Do not stage a "Uploaded to Drive" state without a real connected backend; the app will honestly show "Queued" otherwise.
- Use real keywords and a real short recording for the produce demo.
