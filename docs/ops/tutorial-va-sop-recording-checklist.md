# Tutorial Studio handover recordings

Status: acceptance checklist, not a declaration of production readiness. Record
these with a real VA account only after the corresponding browser journey passes.
Use synthetic tutorials for publishing demonstrations until a real pilot is approved.

## VA recordings

1. **My work and All tutorials:** find assigned work, search the complete history,
   understand stage and next action, and distinguish your channel from a competitor
   source channel. A sole authorized destination is already selected.
2. **Software & topics:** claim a screened keyword, inspect evidence, request
   production once, recover from a lost response without duplicating the tutorial,
   and see production completion reflected in the Keyword Tool. Do not mark a
   production-completed keyword as proof of a public YouTube upload.
3. **Prepare scripts:** review instructions, fix the script, use the workspace voice
   and recipe, and return a bad script for correction rather than recording around it.
4. **Record:** attach the correct English original, preview it, correct a mistaken
   recording, and distinguish processing from a confirmed Drive transfer.
5. **Procedural thumbnails:** choose a layout, edit short English copy, use permanent
   logos/characters/backgrounds, hide an asset from your own collection, restore it,
   select/duplicate/remove layers, and save a draft without approving it. Inspect
   the exported image at small preview size before approval.
6. **AI thumbnails:** review five English candidates, open a full-size quality view,
   inspect quality warnings, refine/regenerate deliberately, and click the chosen
   candidate to approve it. Explain clearly that this click is approval, not merely
   a preview. Localized images derive from that exact English selection. A provider
   outage or an unverified quality check is not a successful generation.
7. **Languages:** compare flagged locale rows, inspect translated headlines and
   thumbnails, correct only the affected language, and understand why an English
   master change invalidates dependent work. Never invent a destination channel.
8. **Final review:** play the actual final video, inspect audio/captions/thumbnail,
   use J/K navigation, learn which approval/rejection shortcuts are enabled, and
   verify that typing into a field does not trigger a review action. An unverified
   automated QA result still needs human review.
9. **Delivery:** confirm the assigned channel and reserved slot. For manual delivery,
   download the approved video, thumbnail and metadata, upload to that channel, then
   report the actual YouTube URL. For connected delivery, inspect the service receipt;
   a queued or planned item is not a published video.
10. **Failures and escalation:** retry the existing operation, keep its identity,
    record the tutorial ID and visible error, and avoid repeatedly generating new
    jobs. Do not change voices, provider keys, global assets or schedules to clear an
    error. Do not delete source media to free disk space.

## Admin recordings

1. Invite/disable VAs, assign channel access, and verify the effective VA view.
2. Switch the global procedural/AI policy; configure VeoForge and demonstrate its
   readiness gate. Verify image capability independently of video capability.
3. Maintain shared references, channel personas, logos, voices and default recipes.
   Distinguish hiding an asset personally from deleting a shared asset globally.
4. Set channel/language schedules, override reservations, and demonstrate emergency
   upload pause and controlled resumption without duplicate publication.
5. Inspect failed provider jobs, bounded quality checks, localization dependencies,
   Drive verification and uploader receipts. Show what evidence permits a retry.
6. Rehearse migration rollback, inspect storage retention, and prove exact Drive
   restoration before enabling any cache eviction. Preserve English originals.

## Cutover acceptance

- A VA completes the entire assigned tutorial workflow without Admin impersonation.
- A duplicate-click and interrupted-request exercise produces no duplicate work.
- English selection, locale lineage, final approval and upload receipt agree.
- Existing accounts, voices and channel reference assets match the source operation.
- Team links point to the chosen production host only after the pilot succeeds.
- Omar's team and assets remain unchanged until their separate migration rehearsal.
